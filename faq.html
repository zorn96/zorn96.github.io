Questions

Do I have to RSVP now?
Only if you don’t believe you will be able to attend. We will be sending out the official RSVP, additional wedding weekend details, and more in the coming months. But if you already know you will be unable to attend, please let us know.
Do I have to be there the entire weekend?
We’d love to see you for as much as you’re willing and able. We understand if that is only the ceremony and reception — we are truly blessed to share our celebration with you regardless.
What is the dress code?
You can find the dress code for each event of the weekend under the Itinerary. As we get closer to the event, we’ll upload reference links to help those who are feeling uncertain about their fashion choices.
Can I bring children?

Although we love your little ones, our wedding ceremony and reception will be an adults-only affair. They are welcome to the Night Before Party and Goodbye Brunch, as well as any activities in between. We are happy to recommend reputable sitter services in the area!

Can I bring a plus one?

Any plus one will be explicitly denoted on your invitation, and you will be able to RSVP for them once you’ve received it.

How can I get updates?
If you fill out the RSVP now with your phone number, email, and general intent to attend, we will send out a text and email when we add new information — including updates on hotel blocks, fine-grained times for events, and more.
